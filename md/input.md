### What I Learned
- "The universe might be indifferent to us, but we are not indifferent to each other." - [John Green](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=--3Qi0Z0ASU)
  - I've been thinking about this when I do tiny things with great love. Ex: Giving a shoutout to someone to a hard working peer, even when I don't have to. The **surprising thing about gratitude is that it costs so little and gives so much.** It might take me 30 seconds to give praise, but it might make someone smile for the rest of the day.
- Some people told me I'm too hard on myself. This concept seems almost nebulous to me. I find it so difficult to 'observe' what being too hard on myself looks like since it's always in ephemary internal thoughts that seemingly pass by without consequence. Even if I do catch these thoughts, what are the effects of these thoughts? It's hard to observe cause and effect relationships when seemingly nothing I can touch, see, sense, etc. has changed. I'm very unsure what to do in response to what people told me.
- Over my time at Waterloo, I've prioritised lots of different things: getting good grades, trying to get research experience, hunting for internships, trying out new clubs like debate, ... Out of all the things I've put my attention on over the past 5 years, the one with the largest impact on my life (and it isn't even CLOSE) is making friends. Whether it comes to my emotional, financial, spiritual, or physical wellbeing - 80% of how I'm doing just comes down to the attenion I invested in building relationships. Good relationships lead me up to success. And when success finds me and all the new people in my life only seem interested in me due to the success, I'll get closer to the older people  that I knew appreciated me for who I was. 
- A model of my mind these days: I have so many questions and so few answers. Whenever I seek out one answer, three more questions appear. This happens whether I'm studying, setting goals, building relationships, etc.
