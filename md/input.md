### What I Learned
- Learned to mealprep for the busy internship ahead. The approach that worked for me:
  1. Filter recipes that can be cooked in 3 hours in one day and reheated in <10 mins throughout the week. Ex: Noodles, oatmeal, stews, etc.
  2. For recipes I like, pick a diverse set of ingredients that have all the vitamins/minerals I need. 
  3. ALWAYS finish seasoning each recipe with premade mixes so my lack of cooking abilities don't ruin the meal in the end :D
