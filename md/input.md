### What I Did
- Started the internship hunting process. It has quite a lot of ups and downs, but it teaches me to have more faith and resilience. It also shows me I have a much stronger support system than I'd first guess.
- Learned a lot when [visiting history museums in Berlin](https://www.madhavmalhotra.com/life-updates/#20260620-204319). I think the most appalling part of the Nazi regime was the 'bureaucracy of evil'. Everyday letters recovered after the war describing war crimes and atrocities. I'd never looked into these everyday details instead of the grand stories we're taught about various battles. It left quite an impression on me. I hope to continue learning about this period of history by visiting the Dachau concentration camp memorial next month.
- Was lucky enough to visit Norway during my European exchange term. There's this one [strikingly beautiful mountain range](https://maps.app.goo.gl/s7ae3ic3r1YkKnVM9) I kept coming back to. I'd still like to go back in the winter season to see it in a different light :D
- Was lucky enough to visit my friend, Darius, in London. More than any other city I've been to, I just immediately [loved the grand architecture around me](https://www.madhavmalhotra.com/life-updates/#20260628-185112). ANY ordinary building near Westminster Abbey would draw crowds for its beauty in North America. Asides from the architecture, I really appreciated the time to talk to Darius. He's a very rare person marked by both extreme intelligence and humility. We had a good conversation on how to prioritise what counts most in life.
