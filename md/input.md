### What I Learned
- I've been growing technically as I learn to identify maintainable code at a much deeper level than just 'is it documented and typed?' Gobbledygook for junior programmers: is all logic contained in one place for easy maintenance? Are there references to other files/functions that could drift/go stale? Are sources of unpredictability (unbounded files, untyped data) narrowed on initial load instead of scattered throughout the codebase? Are abstractions truly independent of data sources or will they require updates to switch data formats/vendors? Can narrow converters suffice to reuse existing components instead of new components with only slight modifications? How have we verified that our functionality fails open or fails loudly? Could we later require concurrency and break/overwrite non-atomic operations?
